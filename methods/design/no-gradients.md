# No gradients

> From Prello, Language App may revise.

Elias, 2026-10-26: "we dont like gradiant, they look AI".

- No linear, radial or conic gradients in UI: buttons, marks, text, surfaces, page washes.
- Flat colours only. Gradient headline words become one plain accent colour.
- Text on a coloured button must pass WCAG AA (4.5:1); pick the darker accent shade if the lighter fails.
- Also applies to film frames: no gradient or glow filler.
- Exception to decide per product: device-mockup shading.
