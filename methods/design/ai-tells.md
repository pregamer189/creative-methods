# AI tells to avoid

AI-made design drifts to the same safe defaults, whatever the subject, so these
read as generated. Don't use one unless the design really needs it, and say why
when you do. Collected from Elias's rejections (2026-10-19 to 10-26) and design
research on generic AI output.

## Layout and decoration
- Everything in identical rounded cards with the same soft grey shadow
  (`rgba(0,0,0,.1)`) and one radius for everything. Use two treatments: a raised
  card for top-level blocks, a flat panel inside; lists are rows with dividers.
- Gradient washes and glows as decoration (`no-gradients.md`).
- A big number, a small label and a gradient accent as the default hero.
- Numbered markers (01, 02, 03) on content that isn't a sequence.
- Fade-and-slide-up entrances on every section; hover lifts on every card.
- Emoji or decorative icons standing in for content.

## Type and labels
- Tracked-out ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels above headings.
- Meta strings joined with middle dots ("A · B · C").
- "Word — fragment" labels with a spaced em dash.
- One word of a headline in a different colour, italic or weight.
- An arrow appended to button text ("Continue →").
- The default font everyone reaches for (Inter, Roboto, the system font alone),
  unless the product has chosen it on purpose.

## Colour
- Warm cream background with a terracotta or coral accent (reads as an AI
  assistant's own look).
- Near-black with one acid-green or vermilion accent.
- Tinted near-black (#0B0B0B, #111) standing in for real ink; pure #000 or #FFF
  for UI neutrals.
- Grey text on a coloured fill.

## Copy
- Selling instead of saying. Buttons say what happens ("Save", "Check"); the same
  action keeps the same name through a flow.
- Guilt and pressure ("Don't lose your streak!").
- Filler adjectives ("seamless", "powerful", "effortless").

## Film
- Logo first, fade from black, a slow build eating the first seconds.
- Same-scene jump cuts, punch-in zooms, shake (`../video/editing-rules.md`).
- Glow filler, generic stock-music beds, captions over the subject.

## Check

Before showing anything: screenshot it, go down this list, then remove one thing
that isn't needed. A product may allow an item on purpose in its own rules (for
example a brand that really uses cream and coral); the product rule wins.
