# Taste as an input

A model can make what it is told, and tends to the average of what it has seen. Taste
has to come from a person and be written down where the model can use it. That is the
part planning can't replace.

## Build the collection (Barney Watts, 450k views)

"Taste is a muscle," and scrolling weakens it. So be picky:

1. Keep only the **rare things that really make you stop**: the piece that sent you
   researching for an hour, the film you still think about a year later.
2. Keep it **tiny**. A small rare collection, not a feed. When you step back from it,
   it should excite you.
3. Write **why** next to each one, in a line. The list without the reasons is useless to
   a model.
4. **Study the masters on purpose.** Skipping them to be different is how you end up
   copying Instagram. Find out what a technique is for: Wong Kar-wai's slow shutter
   isolates his characters; it isn't a fancy transition.

When you later use a technique, it then means something.

Keep a notebook of ideas (Ermia Ramez, Gawx: "most of them are bad, but from time to
time I hit the jackpot").

## Copy the thinking, not the look

"You don't want to look like your heroes. You want to see like your heroes." Mimic only
the surface and the result is a knock-off (Ermia Ramez, drawing on Kleon and Rubin).
Nobody is born with a style; you find it by making things and failing to be your hero.
This is I13 and I59 together: **do what you see in great work, for the reason it was
done.** Balance consuming and making, about 50/50.

## Three levels of taking from references

(Superwall, from growth teams. Written for short-form app marketing.)

| Level | What it means | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Copy | The same format for your product | Fast, saturates first |
| Iterate | Change one variable (hook, caption, script) in something that worked, and watch | Learn what mattered |
| **Referential concepting** | Take a whole creative idea from another field into yours | Riskiest, and how you become the one others copy. Pingo: creators speak badly on camera, its AI roasts them; reaction-video idea brought into language learning |

## File to keep per person or brand

```markdown
# Taste board
| Piece (link, timestamp) | Why it stops me | Technique | What it is for | What we'd take |
```

Marks from reviews go here too: what was loved, tweaked, cut, and why. Read before any
creative brief. It sits with the brand knowledge base in the product's repo
(`../branding/brand-kb.md`), because taste belongs to that brand, not to these methods.

## Check

- [ ] Every item has a reason in a line, written by the person.
- [ ] A new brief names 2-3 items from the board and what to take from each.
- [ ] Nothing in a film is there only because "it looks cool" (Satori: why before what).
