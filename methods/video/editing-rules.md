# Editing rules

> From Prello, Language App may revise.

## No same-scene cuts (Elias, 2026-10-23/24)
- **One image = one shot = one move.** Never cut from a frame back into the same scene or image at another zoom or crop.
- Cut **only on a new action** (something new happens, a new place, a new subject).
- Grounding: cuts are hidden when they land on motion or a new event (edit blindness, Smith & Henderson); a jump inside the same scene reads as a glitch.
- Elias deleted three posted films for breaking this. Treat it as a hard gate.

## Music
- **Banned tracks (never use):** Mixkit 729 "Pop Track 03", Mixkit 158 "Happy Times", Mixkit 726, "garage-24s". "pulse-20s" is not for coded films.
- Pick free, licensed music with **higher BPM (125-140) and real vocals**, a clear hook, a drop and a clean ending (Elias 2026-10-17: soft corporate beds were rejected).
- Measure the track (first downbeat, bars) before timing any cut; beat detection alone misses the first downbeat.
- Open on a strong section, end on the last hit, no silent tail. Duck music 15-20 dB under voice.

## Film types that worked in research (2026-10-24)
A one-take real demo · B before/after · C side-by-side comparison · D founder on camera · E teaser · F user reposts.
