# Principles: how we think before we make anything

The philosophy under every method in this repo. The other folders say how to make
a film, a motion or a page; this folder says how we decide what to make, how we
know it works, and how the work runs from a raw idea to a finished thing.

| File | What it answers |
|---|---|
| `evidence-first.md` | How we decide: sort every question, research before variations, measure real use, rescue what doesn't work |
| `product-philosophy.md` | How a product gets one idea, a page philosophy and rules every screen follows |
| `running-the-work.md` | How the work runs day to day: chunks, plans with a critic pass, decide and log, hard rules from repeated misses |
| `idea-to-finished.md` | The pipeline from a raw idea to a finished scene, video, landing page or screen |

## The beliefs in one screen

1. **Built on what actually works.** There is a lot of evidence out there; use it.
   Don't guess where research can answer, and don't ask the owner what research can
   answer (`evidence-first.md`).
2. **Understanding before variations.** First the research and the data, then the
   options the research leaves open, then variations of those. Never a spread of
   variations to find out what works.
3. **Prove it in use.** Success is what people can really do with the product,
   later, in a new situation. Minutes, taps and streaks are not success.
4. **Show, don't explain.** Structure is felt in the real thing, as a visible
   pattern. One plain line of what it does, and a "Why?" on demand. Long
   explanations break the flow.
5. **Minimal with soul.** Few elements, each with a voice. Plain is not the same as
   minimal; a bare screen reads as unfinished, not calm.
6. **Close to real, never kid-like.** Real material, real light, adult proportions,
   real human voices. Code-drawn imitations of material read as cheap.
7. **Plan much more than you build.** Read every earlier mark first, write a short
   plan, run a critic pass, then build once at the real quality.
8. **Decide, log, keep going.** Research settles most choices. Log the decision and
   its reason, build, and show the owner only finished batches to mark.
9. **A repeated miss becomes a hard rule.** Two misses on the same thing means
   research, then a written rule every later piece is checked against.
10. **The owner's words are kept whole.** Briefs are saved verbatim, every nuance gets
    a line in a checklist, and nothing is called "your idea" without the quote.
