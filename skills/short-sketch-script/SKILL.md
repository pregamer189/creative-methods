# Short sketch script

Version 1.0 (2026-11-20). Changes: CHANGELOG.md in pregamer189/creative-methods.
Sources and their status: SOURCES.md next to this file.

One real person, one phone, one joke. The script is what the person does and
says, second by second. Music timing, shots and cuts belong to `video-storyboard`;
this skill never restates them. The product's own rules (name, voice, what may
be shown) come from its repo and win.

## Rules

1. **One premise, one sentence, "when you...".** One joke per video; a second joke dilutes the first.
2. **The caption does the setup** ("POV...", "when you..."), so the video opens on the turn. Muted viewers still get it.
3. **Start in base reality:** the plain, believable situation in one beat. The viewer must know it in the first second.
4. **Add one unusual thing**, the first thing that is off. Everything funny comes from it.
5. **Name the game in one line:** "if this is true, what else is true?" Play only that.
6. **Heighten in steps, three beats at most**, each a bit bigger or more specific than the last.
7. **End on a button**, a last short line or action, then cut. No wrap-up, no explaining.
8. **Play it real:** the person reacts as a smart real person would, wanting something specific. No mugging, no pretending not to know what they know.
9. **Write every reaction as an action with a want and a length in seconds.** A pose is not a reaction.
10. **No reaction over 2 s without a new action.** A held face reads as a pose, then as a threat.
11. **Silence under 1.5 s.** A beat is short; longer needs a movement or a sound inside it.
12. **Inner thoughts go on screen as short text**, not as a long stare to camera. Each line stays up long enough to read (letters / 15 + 1.5 s, at least 1.8 s), so keep lines to a few words.
13. **Be specific:** a named thing, number, brand-free detail beats "something awkward". The viewer should say "that's me".
14. **Punch at yourself or the situation**, never at the other person or their culture. A stranger must find it warm.
15. **Read it aloud and time it** with a stopwatch before filming. Cut anything you stumble on.

## Template

```markdown
**Premise ("when you..."):**
**Base reality (one beat):**
**The unusual thing:**
**The game (one line):**
**Beats (max 3):** 1 / 2 / 3
**Button:**
**Reactions:** each one = action + what they want + seconds (none over 2 s without a new action)
**On-screen text:** caption (setup), inner-thought lines, timing of each
**What the viewer keeps or learns:** one line
**Length:** __ s at a normal speaking pace (5 to 20 s)
```

## Creepy check (all must pass)

- [ ] No held smile; a smile starts and ends in motion
- [ ] No stare into the lens (a look to camera is one quick glance, under 1 s)
- [ ] No silence over 1.5 s
- [ ] No slow nod, slow turn or slow smile
- [ ] Every reaction is a movement (breath, glance, hand, step), not a pose
- [ ] Stranger test: would someone who knows nothing find it warm or weird? Weird means rewrite

## Good and bad

- Bad (failed, 2026-11-20; the owner: "that sounds like a horror film"): someone
  says the one sentence they practised in another language, a barista
  replies fast, then "hold still and smile for six seconds, let the smile
  freeze, then say yes". Frozen smile, six seconds, no want, no new action: a
  horror film, and the joke ("I understood none of it") never lands.
- Good: caption "When you practised one sentence and they answer at full speed."
  Beat 1 (1 s): says the sentence, proud. Beat 2 (2 s): barista's fast reply as
  audio; he nods quick, eyes tracking the words, catching none. Beat 3 (2 s):
  he picks up one word he knows and answers it, "yes!", too loud. Button
  (2 s): text "(I ordered nothing)" and he takes the cup anyway; cut.
- Bad: "He looks confused for a while." No action, want or seconds.

## With the other film skills

- `video-storyboard` owns shots, cuts, music and the muted first 2 s. Its held
  frames and near-silence are allowed, but a person's face never holds longer
  than rules 10 and 11 here.
- A sketch carries no ask inside it; the ask, if any, goes in the post text.
- `film-quality-gate` checks the finished film; its text hold time is rule 12.

## Done when

The template is filled with one premise, at most three beats and a button; every
reaction has an action, a want and seconds; the creepy check is ticked; it was
read aloud and fits 5 to 20 s; a stranger test found it warm. Then plan shots
and timing with `video-storyboard`.
