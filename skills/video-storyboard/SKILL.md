# Video storyboard

Plan the whole film on one page before anything is built, so nothing is
improvised and every note can point at one shot.

## Rules

- **One idea per film**, said in one sentence. Two good ideas = two films.
- **A film is a trust arc**, not a pile of likeable things. Name the one step this
  film moves: curiosity → understanding → belief → action.
- **A story** has structure (beginning, turn, end), a subject, and development
  (something changes).
- **First 2 s work muted:** the real thing doing something, or text that lands. No
  logo first, no fade from black.
- **One image = one shot = one move.** A new shot only on a new action. Never cut
  back into the same image at another zoom or crop.
- **Music first.** Measure the first downbeat and bar length; shot times sit on bars.
- **Every visual event has a sound.**
- **Every claim is true on a real screen today.**
- Learn from great references by their reasons (pacing, reveal, what viewers said
  they loved), never by copying shots.
- The product's own rules (name, palette, fonts, banned music, what may be
  generated) come from its repo and win.

## Shapes

| Shape | Beats | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Framing, demo, closing | Problem line, product doing it, one action | Launch, 30-45 s |
| Before and after | Old way, turn, new way | A visible result |
| One-take demo | A real task start to finish | Proof |
| Maker story | Why, one honest moment, what's next | Build in public |
| Side by side | Same task two ways | Comparison |

## Template

```markdown
# NNN · Title

**One idea (one sentence):**
**Trust step:** curiosity / understanding / belief / action
**Shape:**
**Length and format:** e.g. 35 s, 9:16 master + 16:9
**Look:** the product's tokens (two main colours); flat fills, no gradients
**Music:** tempo, mood, track; first downbeat at __ s, bar = __ s
**Score:** outside __ · owner: like / unsure / dislike

## Shots
| # | Time (bars) | Shot (wide / close / action / screen) | Move | What happens | Text or voice | Sound | Cut out (axis, direction, what carries the eye) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Assets
Per shot: exists / to make / needs a yes before generating.

## Check before showing
- [ ] Hook works muted in 2 s
- [ ] One idea only
- [ ] Every claim true today
- [ ] No same-scene cuts; 2-3 transition types
- [ ] Product rules hold
- [ ] An outside eye watched it
```

## Good and bad

- Good: "Shot 3, bars 5-6: the card flips to the answer; tick sound; cut out on the
  flip's direction (left), the next card enters moving left."
- Bad: "Shot 3: show the app looking great." No action, no time, no sound.
- Bad: shot 4 is shot 3 zoomed in. That's a same-scene cut.

## Done when

The page has one sentence, one trust step, every shot timed on bars with a sound
and a cut plan, and the check list is ticked. Then build, then run the film
quality gate.
