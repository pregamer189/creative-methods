# Full text through OneSearch (Elias's school library, on his Mac)

For a paper the open sources only give as an abstract, and that a plan really
rests on. Everything else stays on the free tools.

1. Find the DOI with `search_papers`; `paper_details` says "No free full text found".
2. Ask Elias once, in the project chat, with the title and why it matters.
3. On his Mac (a Remote Control session or the browser tools, folder
   `~/LanguageApp`): open the library's OneSearch from the start page he is already
   signed in to. **He signs in himself**; the agent never types or stores his
   school login.
4. Search the DOI, open the full text, save the PDF to
   `~/LanguageApp/research/papers/<doi with / as _>.pdf`. That folder stays on his
   Mac: licensed PDFs are never committed or uploaded.
5. Read it there and write what matters into the research brief, with page
   numbers and short quotes, citing the DOI.
