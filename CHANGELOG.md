# Changelog

Every change to a skill in `skills/` adds a line here and bumps the skill's
`Version` line. The **Re-paste** column says whether account skills pasted into
claude.ai need updating (yes for any rule change; no for typo-only fixes).
Methods without a skill are listed too, so product repos know what moved.

Versions: `major.minor`. Minor = a rule added, changed or clarified. Major = the
skill's job or steps changed, so earlier output may no longer pass.
